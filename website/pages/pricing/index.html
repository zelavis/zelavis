Questions.

Is Zelavis still free if I self-host? +

Yes. The platform is open source and the Community plan is the full self-hosted product — every storage driver, native website hosting, workloads, and the Marketplace. Zelavis Plus pays for us to run and operate it for you.

Can I still get Zelavis Plus if I self-host? +

Yes. Zelavis Plus works whether Zelavis is self-hosted or hosted for you on Zelavis Cloud. If you prefer self-hosting, you can keep Zelavis where it is and add the five Plus pillars. If you prefer a fully managed experience, we can host and operate the complete platform for you.

What happens to my data if I leave Plus? +

Projects are portable by design. Every project locks its exact recipe version and owns its own data directory, so a managed project can be exported and started on your own hardware without a rewrite.

Do you charge per project? +

No. Plus is priced per seat, and projects are unlimited on every plan.

When do planning and collaboration arrive? +

Project planning and real-time collaboration are committed roadmap direction rather than shipped features today. They are not part of what any plan bills for right now, and pricing for them will be announced before they ship.
